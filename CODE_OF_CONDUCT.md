# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Technical disagreements are welcome;
harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, and personal attacks are
not. Respect people's privacy and do not post their credentials or server data.

Keep feedback specific to the work, accept corrections, and help newcomers.
Maintainers may remove abusive content or restrict participation, proportionate
to the behavior. Enforcement must be fair and consistent.

The public repository and its private reporting contact have not been created.
Publishing a monitored private conduct-reporting channel is a prerequisite for
opening the project to public contributions.
